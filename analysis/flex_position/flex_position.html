Method. Starters come from each league's MFL league endpoint (starters); scoring is MFL's own reported points per player-week (playerScores) for that league, regular season to date. MFL gives a min-max range per position and a total per side, so fixed slots = the minimum at each position and flex slots = side total − sum of minimums; only positions whose max exceeds their min can take flex. The fixed line at a position is teams × its minimum. The flex band is the next depth = teams × flex slots × the depth slider players past that line (default: exactly the players who end up in flex). The flex pool is the best remaining eligible players, taking at most (max − min) × teams at any one position. PPG = points / games played (a game = a non-zero score). The pool is every NFL player with the minimum games, owned or not; positions are MFL's own for that player id. Recomputed live in this page, nothing is averaged across rates.
